Mushrooms & Rock’n’Roll

A WHOLE RESTAURANT DEDICATED TO ONE SINGLE INGREDIENT: MUSHROOMS.

Since 2013, every dish — from the couvert to dessert — has revolved around mushrooms: wild or cultivated, fresh or dried.

Our cooking has Italian roots, but stateless freedom and local ingredients. We treat each species with curiosity, technique and the respect it deserves: from wild Boletus, chanterelles, morels, Caesar’s mushrooms and black trumpets to cultivated Agaricus, shiitake and oyster mushrooms. And we have a beautiful view over the Tagus!

(We do not, however, have magic mushrooms. Sorry 🙂)
